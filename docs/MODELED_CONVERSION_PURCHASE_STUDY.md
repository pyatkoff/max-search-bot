# Prospective outcome comparison

This study compares existing early signals with the same signals plus travel
conditions. Its target is a manager-recorded won/lost inquiry outcome within
thirty days. Measurements describe the resolved subset of inquiries.

Features, models and predictions remain frozen. A later read confirms a saved
prediction before it becomes eligible for outcome comparison. Outcomes recorded
at or before that confirmation are excluded. Missing, open and immature outcomes
remain unknown.

Both feature sets use the same learner and eligible inquiries. Paired scores and
uncertainty intervals measure whether the extra conditions improve forecasts.
Real-data advantage requires mature prospective evidence. Monetary export stays
disabled.

The regression suites are `tests/run_purchase_conversion_study.py` and
`tests/run_purchase_study_runtime.py`.
